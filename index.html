About Rannaghar

A small home kitchen in Bally with one big goal: to serve you the comforting, authentic taste of Bengali home cooking.

Made Like It Is At Home

Rannaghar started with a simple belief: the best Bengali food is the kind cooked at home. We do not use mass-production shortcuts or frozen bases. Every masala is ground fresh, every curry is simmered slowly, and every plate is packed with the same care we would serve our own family.

From Kosha Mangsho on a Sunday afternoon to Shorshe Ilish when the season is right, our menu celebrates the everyday food Bengali households love.
